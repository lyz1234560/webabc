About Me

Most of Baoding is plain, which is great for radio. However, I live in Laiyuan County – the highest average altitude in Baoding – and the county seat is a mountain basin. There are 12 amateur repeaters in Baoding, but Laiyuan doesn't have one. The nearest one is in Yuxian, Zhangjiakou, 50 km away. (Hover over the image)

Because of this, I got my license at the beginning of summer but haven't made any contacts by the end of summer. I'm learning satellite communication, so if you contact me, it will likely be via amateur satellite.
