About Me

Most of Baoding is plain, which is of course very suitable for ham radio. However, I live in Laiyuan County, which has the highest average elevation in Baoding and the county seat is a mountain basin. There are 12 amateur repeaters in Baoding, but Laiyuan County doesn't have one. The nearest amateur repeater is 50km away in Yuxian County, Zhangjiakou.(Try to remove mouse over the picture)

That's why I got my license at the beginning of summer but haven't made a single QSO by the end of summer. I've tried many methods during this time. I tried to make a YAGI antenna, but was tortured by its extremely unstable performance… When I wanted to work satellites, the terrifying speaking speed of the masters on the satellites made me lose courage to transmit (of course my 771 antenna couldn't reach it either).

I'm learning satellite communications, so if you QSO with me, chances are it will be via amateur satellite~
